Your Questions

Before you decide

Is this a coaching program or a real business? +
Both. You get training and support AND a clear path to build income in financial services — systems, a 90-day roadmap, licensing guidance, weekly targets, and accountability all in one place.
Do I need any experience to start? +
Zero. Most members came in with no financial background, no sales experience, and no idea where to begin. The system is built specifically for people starting from scratch.
How many hours per week do I realistically need? +
7–10 hours a week is enough to build momentum part-time. 10–15 hours per week will accelerate the process significantly. This isn't a 40-hour grind — it's consistent daily reps.
Is this MLM? What's the actual difference? +
This is a licensed financial services business. You get paid by product carriers for helping clients — not for signing people up. Your income can be built 100% through clients alone.
How do I actually get paid? +
When a client chooses a financial solution — like life insurance or investments — the product carrier pays you a commission. The client doesn't pay you directly.
What if I don't have a big network? +
You don't need one. We give you a step-by-step process and a built-in system designed to get you conversations even as a complete beginner. Once licensed, your pipeline grows with automated follow-up.
