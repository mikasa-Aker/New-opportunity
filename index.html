Your Questions

Before you decide

Is this just a coaching program or a real business? +
Both. You get training and support AND a clear path to build income in financial services. We give you systems, a 90-day roadmap, licensing guidance, weekly targets, and accountability — all together in one place.
Is this about selling insurance? +
Insurance is one of the licensed solutions you'll offer — but it's not the only one. You'll also learn investments, debt strategies, and practical money tools that help families in meaningful, tangible ways.
Do I need any experience to start? +
Zero. Most members came in with no financial background, no sales experience, and no idea where to begin. The system is built specifically for people starting from scratch.
How many hours per week do I realistically need? +
7–10 hours a week is enough to build momentum part-time if you follow the plan consistently. 10–15 hours per week will accelerate the process. This isn't a 40-hour grind — it's a business built through consistent daily reps: conversations, follow-ups, and booking calls.
Is this MLM? What's the actual difference? +
This is a licensed financial services business. You get paid by product carriers for helping clients — not for signing people up. Your income can be built 100% through clients alone. Real products, real regulation, real consumer demand.
How do I actually get paid? +
When a client chooses a financial solution you help them set up — like life insurance, investments, or debt solutions — the product carrier pays you a commission. The client doesn't pay you directly. If you build a team later, there can also be additional income from supporting other agents.
What if I don't have a big network or don't feel confident? +
You don't need a big network. We give you a step-by-step process and a built-in system designed to get you conversations even as a complete beginner. Once licensed, your pipeline grows with automated follow-up — so it compounds even when you're not actively working it.
